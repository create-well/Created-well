🌿 CO-CREATE WELL WORKSHOP TASK FLOW
Last updated: Jul 3, 2025
We can adapt the timeline for shorter notice, but the intention of this working structure ensures sustainability, content quality, and strong turnout.
Based on: ​​Posing Workshop Logistics + Marketing Template

🌀 PHASE 0: ALIGNMENT & INTENTIONAL CURATION
Timeline: 6–8 weeks before event/workshop
A. Mission + Values Alignment with Co-Hoes & Co-Creator(s)/Facilitator(s)
* Share our Create Well Mission & Values Doc (Future Deck)
   * CREATE WELL CO-CREATOR MANUAL
   * Clarify pillars like flowing over forcing, creative restoration, intuition, community care, and anti-hustle values and shitttttt
   * Clarify what Create Well is and isn’t (e.g. Not productivity coaching, not over-structured, not a place for healing - but instead a place for nurturing)
* Host an Alignment Call with each potential co-creator (facilitator or collaborator)
   * Clarify energetic compatibility, shared intentions, and spaceholding style
   * Ask: “What does creative wellness mean to you?” and “What do you want participants to feel after your workshop?”
   * How does the workshop embody Creative Wellness? Practical takeaways and application after the workshop etc.
B. Workshop Intent + Role Clarity
* Invite them to submit a Workshop Vision Outline/Sketch (Email or Google Doc?)
   * Draft title/theme + core transformation statement (inner/outer expression?)
   * Workshop format 
   * Target audience + core takeaway, practical application
   * Optional: upsell or CTA (e.g. 1:1 sessions, membership, product)
* Define role expectations and co-creation boundaries:
   * Who is lead vs support (content creation, setup, teaching, follow-up)
   * Who owns/creates visual assets, deck design, worksheet creation
   * Identify needed support from Create Well (AV, photographer, budget, etc.)
________________


C. Capacity, Boundaries, and Agreements
* Clarify mutual capacity and timing preferences:
   * Discuss calendar compatibility and emotional availability
   * Include soft boundaries for energy protection and pacing (e.g. “no last-minute marketing rush”)
* Confirm revenue model and deliverables:
   * Profit split for Muse In Motion, 
   * In-kind barter/gift-based exchange (studio included)
      * Added special/upsell for facilitators: photoshoot with Muse In Motion or video services from Angela (brand video)
      * Facilitator exchange included: studio time + content/marketing type shit
   * Marketing contribution (e.g. 2 IG posts + 1 email minimum)
   * Deadlines for materials (slides, images, worksheets) - outlined later in this doc
* Finalize Agreement in writing/documented somewhere for clarity:
   * Include workshop logistics, expectations, and timeline summary
   * Add to internal workshop tracker with facilitator bio + contact info
   * Determine how to handle cancellations and/or postponements[a]
      * Hard start for marketing ~4 weeks out
         * Secure date at LEAST one month before
      * Swap? Reschedule? Define
________________


🔮 PHASE 1: STRATEGY + CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT
Timeline: 5–6 weeks before event/workshop
1. Create Well Workshop Alignment & Audience Clarity
* Write a 1-liner transformation statement:
Ex: “From camera-shy to confidently captured.”
   * (Is “transformation” the right word? I don’t want people feeling like who they are rn isn’t worthy or valuable. Maybe something like “metamorphosis” LOL that’s the same. But something STICKYYYYY)[b]
   * Define top 2–3 ideal participant profiles:
   * Creative entrepreneur in transition
   * Business owner rebranding themselves
   * Corporate professional seeking embodiment & presence
   * Draft 2 pain point statements per profile to use in marketing copy
2. Format, Theme & Structure
   * Set preliminary date & backup date
   * Decide:
   * Duration (e.g. 2.5 or 3 hrs)
   * Capacity cap (e.g. 12 max)
   * Format: 60% teaching, 30% activity, 10% CTA
   * E.g. posing presentation, posing practice, mini shoot
   * Confirm if food/drinks are included[c] (if so, outline sourcing plan)
3. Venue Prep: Sunshine Digital Content Studios
   * Prep studio with lighting, seating, content A/V
   * Prepare standard info:
   * Parking instructions
   * Entrance process
   * Set-up/clean-up needs
________________


🎯 PHASE 2: MARKETING + FUNNEL PREP
Timeline: 4–5 weeks before event
4. Set Up Registration & Ticketing
   * Create registration page on:
   * Posh (main platform)
   * Eventbrite (for further reach & SEO)
   * Partiful (for mass text invites)
   * Website
   * Add RSVP email + mobile collection
   * Standardized questions: 
- “What are you hoping to gain?”
- “Do you consent to photos/videos being taken for marketing purposes?”
      * Confirm connection to payment processor: Stripe / Venmo / PayPal
      * Set early bird deadline with automatic price increase
      * Optional: Offer “Pay it forward” or sponsored slot ticket
5. Design Marketing Materials
      * Align and write copy:
      * Workshop name + subtitle
      * 2–3 hook/tagline options (“Not a model? Perfect.”)
      * 150-word event description (optimized and easily used/repurposed for Posh, Instagram, Eventbrite, LinkedIn, etc)
      * Design:
      * Instagram posts (square + vertical)
      * Reel script outline (introduce workshop in under 30s)
      * Countdown story templates (Canva / IG)
      * “Meet the Facilitators” posts
      * Other Create Well coded templates?
      * Export visuals with alt text, file names for SEO (e.g. “confident_on_camera_workshop.jpg”) - suggestion from AI fwends
6. Draft Launch Content Plan
      * Email: 1 announcement, 1 reminder, 1 final call
      * Social:
      * IG feed post (launch)
      * Reel (hook or teaser)
      * Countdown stories (every 5–7 days)
      * Threads/X: Quote tweets about workshop themes
      * LinkedIn post (for professional tone)
      * Facebook Event page if relevant
7. Direct Outreach Strategy
      * DM / email past attendees: personalized invite with link
      * Outreach to 3 complementary businesses or communities to cross-share
      * Ex: local coworking space, mental health collective, photography group
      * Let’s make a list!
      * Add event to local FB groups, Meetup, Substack community posts
________________


🛠️ PHASE 3: OPERATIONS + EVENT LOGISTICS
Timeline: 2–4 weeks before event
8. Assign Team Roles
      * Lead facilitator (co-creator)
      * Co-creators, co-facilitators or vibe support (besides us)
      * Video/Photographer / AV support
      * Door / check-in person
      * Tech (laptop, tethering, speaker if relevant)
      * TestiMONNY moments?
9. Set Up Internal Folder System (Google Drive)
      * Folder structure:
      * [WORKSHOP NAME]
      * Marketing
      * Photos
      * Feedback
      * Registration/RSVPs
      * Decks / Worksheets / Resources
      * Share folder with team + create file submission deadlines
10. Prep Materials + Tech
      * Print/Export:
      * Worksheets
      * Name tags
      * Pose cards or cue decks (if relevant)
      * Feedback forms (QR and/or paper)

         * Tech needs:
         * Prep any needs according to workshop co-creator
         * E.g. tethering software test (if using live photo)
         * Lighting or ring light test, mic/video set-up
11. Confirm Reminders + Communication
         * Send reminder emails:
         * 5 days before (what to bring, parking, what to expect)
         * 1 day before (logistics + how to prep mentally)
         * Set SMS reminder via Posh (if poss) / Eventbrite / Partiful 
________________


📸 PHASE 4: WORKSHOP DAY
Timeline: Event day!!!
12. Example Run-of-Show Structure
         * 30 min early arrival for setup
         * Check-in with photo consent form (name tags?)
         * Monny leads opening grounding exercise (somatic, breath, optional name-share & transition to lead)
         * Workshop flow:
         * Intro / values
         * Core content (slide + story)
         * Pair activity or embodied reflection
         * Final integration activity (e.g. mini shoots, journaling)
         * CTA or upsell (membership, follow-up session, etc.)
         * Collect feedback via QR or physical forms
13. Capture Content
         * Have a shot list for:
         * Behind the scenes (setup, interaction)
         * Activity in motion
         * Reflection moments
         * Testimonnyyyy moments post-activity or wherever relebant
         * Capture 3–5 short vertical videos for post-Reels & youtube shorts
________________


🎞️ PHASE 5: POST-PRODUCTION + CO-HOES LEGACY
Timeline: 1–7 days after
14. Deliver Value to Participants
         * Send thank-you email (w/ feedback link)
         * Deliver:
         * Headshots or digital downloads
         * Any handouts or replays
         * Special invite to next offering or discount
         * Perhaps we consider: Add participants to a private group/chat[d] (Discord has been a game changer for FE!) 
15. Edit & Schedule Post-Event Content
         * Schedule:
         * Workshop recap post with photo highlights
         * 2 Reels (1 serious, 1 humorous silly cute “What do I do with my hands?”)
         * Written reflection post (from you or a participant)
16. Archive & Reflect
         * Add notes from workshop in feedback tracker
         * Debrief with team: what worked, what to change
         * Record metrics:
         * Total registered vs attended
         * Profit/loss breakdown
         * Clicks and conversion (optional)
